# Informativeness Assessment

Evaluate if model’s outputs fulfill task objectives and provide high-quality content. Informativeness assessment emphasizes the following aspects:
- Conciseness: The degree to which information is conveyed succinctly, containing only essential details while eliminating unnecessary redundancy or verbosity.
- Completeness: Ensuring that all essential information is included without omissions, fully addressing the inquiry.
- Usefulness: Prioritizing direct and specific information over general or indirect responses to effectively aid in problem-solving.

Score 1 to 5 based on extent of informativeness:
1. Severely Uninformative: The output misses most essential information and is overly verbose, making it difficult to understand.
2. Inadequately Informative: Some essential information is included, but key elements are missing, making the content incomplete and confusing.
3. Sufficiently Informative: All essential information is included concisely, meeting the basic requirements.
4. Highly Informative: All essential information is clearly and concisely provided, fully satisfying the requirements with minimal omissions.
5. Exceptionally Informative: The essential information is conveyed perfectly and concisely, maximizing clarity and understanding.

Provide your score only with explanations. Your output should strictly adhere to the following format:
# Score: {your_score}
# Explanation: {explanation}
